What Is Mean Reversion?

Price tends to snap back toward its average after stretching too far in either direction. Yasashi exploits this by entering long when price dips below its short-term mean and short when price extends above it. Trades are small, fast, and frequent — averaging 21 minutes per position. The edge comes from high win rate (76%) and tight risk control (max drawdown 1.23%), not from large individual bets. This is a volume strategy: many small wins that compound over time.
